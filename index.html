Investing for Good
Driving Growth and Positive Change

Rooted in Values, Guided by Vision, Committed to Progress



Param Hansa Values is Swapna and Dheeraj Pandey's private investment firm.

At Param Hansa Values, we believe in investing for good – driving growth, innovation, and positive change. Our approach combines direct investments in public and private companies with indirect capital allocations to funds, venture capital, and private equity.

We actively support businesses at all stages of their growth journey, from early and emerging startups to late-stage growth equity ventures. By making anchor investments and co-investing with leading venture capital firms, we help unlock potential and foster sustainable progress.





Param Hansa derives the name from Paramhansa Yogananda and Values refers to his values as well as the principles of Value Investing by Ben Graham.

We are inspired by their wisdom, while grounded in value investing.
