Philosophy

At Param Hansa Values, our investment philosophy is rooted in creating long-term value for both our partners and the communities we impact, ensuring every decision aligns with our mission to invest responsibly and for the greater good.



Our Vision


To embark on an endless journey of learning and innovation, collaborating with visionary thinkers, passionate problem solvers, and entrepreneurial leaders.

We aim to support institutions that prioritize workplace equity and life science companies committed to enhancing the quality of human life with a long-term perspective on creating meaningful impact.



Our Mission


Harnessing the power of incremental improvements and compounding to achieve sustainable long-term growth.

Embracing the time-tested principles of value investing to uncover opportunities for large-scale impact.

Driving innovation at the intersection of biology and computer science to deepen our understanding of the cell, accelerate drug discovery, and advance breakthroughs in gene, cell, and immunotherapy.
