Leadership Team


Dheeraj Pandey      

Technopreneur, Philanthropist & Co-founder - Param Hansa Values

Dheeraj Pandey is an Indian-born American entrepreneur, CEO, and philanthropist. As the Chairman and CEO of DevRev Inc., a SaaS company focused on AI-driven software and customer engineering workflows, Dheeraj pioneers innovation. Previously, he co-founded Nutanix, Inc. and served as its CEO until December 2020.

Dheeraj holds a B.Tech in Computer Science from IIT Kanpur and an M.S. in Computer Science from University of Texas, Austin. He is also a board member of Adobe Inc.

Alongside his wife, Swapna Pandey, he founded Param Hansa Philanthropies, aiming to enhance human life through science and tech-driven initiatives. Their foundation's inaugural commitment of $10M to UT Austin in 2020 bolstered computational oncology research. With his vast tech industry experience and philanthropic vision, Dheeraj drives Param Hansa Philanthropies towards global impact.






Amit Kumar   

Investment Professional, Co-founder & CIO - Param Hansa Values

Amit Kumar is the Chief Investment Officer at Param Hansa Values, focused on private and public investments primarily in healthcare and technology industry. His investment interests are in semiconductors, software, life sciences, and biotech. Previously, Amit co-managed over $2 billion in U.S. equity funds at Columbia Threadneedle for over six years. Over two decades of investing career, Amit has invested in high quality companies with strong product leadership across semiconductor chip design, capital equipment, enterprise software, robotic surgery, and gene therapy.

Prior to his financial career, Amit worked in the tech industry for nearly a decade. He holds an MBA from Columbia Business School, New York, and a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering from IIT (BHU).






Vinodh Nalluri   

Director of Research

As the Director of Research at Param Hansa Values, Vinodh brings over two decades of global equities investing experience. He previously founded and managed Sage Hill Capital LP and Dhara Partners LP, leveraging value investing principles. Earlier, he served as Vice President and research analyst at First Eagle Global Value Fund, a New York based investment firm managing assets exceeding $100 billion.

As a generalist, Vinodh has covered diverse sectors including TMT, Consumer Products, Real Estate, Industrials, and more. Prior to his career in finance, he gained product design and development expertise as a semiconductor engineer at Texas Instruments, Dallas.

Vinodh holds an MBA from Columbia Business School, New York, where he was part of the value investing program, and an MSEE in Semiconductor IC Design from Arizona State University.






Rakesh Kumar   

Director - Operations

Rakesh Kumar is the Director of Operations with Param Hansa Values. With nearly three decades of experience in both non-profit and for-profit sectors, Rakesh oversees the business processes to ensure productivity and efficiency.

A professional social worker, Rakesh holds training from the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai, and is a distinguished fellow of the Packard-Gates Population Leadership Program at the University of Washington, Seattle. He also oversees global operations for Param Hansa Philanthropies, the Family Foundation of Swapna and Dheeraj Pandey.
